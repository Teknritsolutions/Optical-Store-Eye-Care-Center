THE NUMBERS, EXPLAINED

Find a fit that feels right.

Frame measurements are a starting point. A fitting considers how the frame sits on your nose, cheeks and ears.

  • Lens width: horizontal size of one lens
  • Bridge: the distance between the lenses
  • Temple: the arm length behind your ears
